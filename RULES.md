Here is a comprehensive, stand‑alone document of the **Risiko (Risk)** rules, written in clear, step‑by‑step form. I base this on the official Italian and international rule‑sets, so it matches the classic “World Domination” version you get in the standard box. [cdn.1j1ju](https://cdn.1j1ju.com/medias/ad/d9/a3-risk-rulebook.pdf)

***

## 1. Goal of the game

The goal is to **conquer the world** by eliminating all the opponents’ armies from the board. [wikihow](https://www.wikihow.com/Play-Risk)
Depending on the variant, you can also win by completing a **secret mission** (e.g., “control 18 territories” or “eliminate a specific color”). [media.wizards](https://media.wizards.com/2015/downloads/ah/Risk_rules.pdf)

***

## 2. Components

- Game board with **42 territories** grouped into **6 continents**. [risiko](https://risiko.it/wp-content/uploads/2017/10/Regolamento-Risiko.pdf)
- Colored **army tokens** (infantry, cavalry, artillery – each with a point value when traded in for reinforcements). [risiko](https://risiko.it/wp-content/uploads/2017/10/Regolamento-Risiko.pdf)
- **Risk cards** (with territory names and symbols: infantry, cavalry, artillery, and “wildcards”). [media.wizards](https://media.wizards.com/2015/downloads/ah/Risk_rules.pdf)
- **2 dice** (usually one red and one white, or two standard dice). [cdn.1j1ju](https://cdn.1j1ju.com/medias/ad/d9/a3-risk-rulebook.pdf)

***

## 3. Setup

### 3.1. Choose number of players and colors

- **2–6 players** can play. [wikihow](https://www.wikihow.com/Play-Risk)
- Each player chooses a color and takes the matching army tokens. [risiko](https://risiko.it/wp-content/uploads/2017/10/Regolamento-Risiko.pdf)

### 3.2. Initial territory setup

The standard rule is:

1. Territorial setup by **lot (random draw)**:  
   - All 42 territories are shuffled and dealt out among the players.  
   - Each player places **1 army** on each territory they receive. [media.wizards](https://media.wizards.com/2015/downloads/ah/Risk_rules.pdf)

2. If not all territories are dealt:
   - Remaining territories are left empty (no one controls them). [risiko](https://risiko.it/wp-content/uploads/2017/10/Regolamento-Risiko.pdf)

Alternative common setup (especially in quick games):  
- Each player places **3 armies** on the board in successive rounds, starting clockwise, until all initial armies are placed. [scribd](https://www.scribd.com/document/587952640/Regolamento-Risiko-it-En)

***

## 4. Receiving starting armies

Right after the initial placement, each player receives **additional starting armies** to place on their own territories:

- **3 players**: 35 armies each  
- **4 players**: 30 armies each  
- **5 players**: 25 armies each  
- **6 players**: 20 armies each  

These are distributed in **rounds of 3 armies at a time**, clockwise, until each player’s total is placed.  
- You can split the 3 armies over several territories if you want. [scribd](https://www.scribd.com/document/587952640/Regolamento-Risiko-it-En)

***

## 5. Turn structure

On each turn, a player performs **three phases in order**:

1. **Reinforcements**  
2. **Attack** *(optional)*  
3. **Fortification / Strategic move** *(optional)*  

You must finish all phases before play passes to the next player. [media.wizards](https://media.wizards.com/2015/downloads/ah/Risk_rules.pdf)

***

## 6. Phase 1: Reinforcements

At the start of your turn, you receive **new armies** that you can place on any of your territories (one territory at a time).  

You get reinforcements from **three sources**:

### 6.1. By number of territories controlled

- **1 army for each 3 territories** you control, rounded down.  
  Example: 17 territories → 5 armies \(\left\lfloor 17/3 \right\rfloor = 5\). [risiko](https://risiko.it/wp-content/uploads/2017/10/Regolamento-Risiko.pdf)

### 6.2. By continents controlled

- For each **entire continent** you control (all its territories), you get the **continent bonus** printed on the board.  
  Example: Asia = 7 armies, Europe = 5 armies, etc. [media.wizards](https://media.wizards.com/2015/downloads/ah/Risk_rules.pdf)

### 6.3. By trading in Risk cards

- You can trade in **sets of Risk cards** at the start of your turn (before attacking).  
- A valid set is:
  - **3 cards of the same type** (3 infantry, 3 cavalry, or 3 artillery), or  
  - **3 cards of all different types** (1 infantry + 1 cavalry + 1 artillery), or  
  - **2 identical symbols + 1 wild** (wild can substitute any type). [risiko](https://risiko.it/wp-content/uploads/2017/10/Regolamento-Risiko.pdf)

- The **number of armies** you get for a set increases as more sets are traded in the game:
  - 1st set: 4 armies  
  - 2nd set: 6 armies  
  - 3rd set: 8 armies  
  - 4th set: 10 armies  
  - 5th set: 12 armies  
  - 6th set: 15 armies  
  - From 7th set onward: add 5 armies to the previous set (15+5, 20+5, etc.). [cdn.1j1ju](https://cdn.1j1ju.com/medias/ad/d9/a3-risk-rulebook.pdf)

- **Important**:  
  - You must trade **exactly 3 cards** at a time.  
  - You can only trade cards **once per turn**, at the **beginning**, before attacking. [media.wizards](https://media.wizards.com/2015/downloads/ah/Risk_rules.pdf)
  - After trade‑in, draw a new Risk card (see section 9).  

***

## 7. Phase 2: Attack

You may attack **any number of times** during your attack phase, as long as you have at least **2 armies** in the attacking territory and can legally attack a neighboring enemy territory. [risiko](https://risiko.it/wp-content/uploads/2017/10/Regolamento-Risiko.pdf)

### 7.1. Which territories can attack which?

- An attack can start **only from a territory you control** and target **one neighboring territory** (connected by a line on the board) that is **controlled by an opponent**. [media.wizards](https://media.wizards.com/2015/downloads/ah/Risk_rules.pdf)

- You cannot attack:
  - Another of your own territories.  
  - A territory that is not adjacent.  
  - A territory that is empty (no one is there). [risiko](https://risiko.it/wp-content/uploads/2017/10/Regolamento-Risiko.pdf)

### 7.2. How to attack

1. **Declare the attack**  
   - Choose the attacking territory and the target territory.  
   - Choose how many armies to attack with (**minimum 1**, maximum equal to the number of armies in the attacking territory minus 1, because you must leave at least 1 behind). [media.wizards](https://media.wizards.com/2015/downloads/ah/Risk_rules.pdf)

2. **Roll the dice**

   - The **attacker** rolls **up to 3 dice**, but the number of dice cannot exceed the number of armies attacking.  
     Example: 4 attacking armies → can roll 1, 2, or 3 dice. [risiko](https://risiko.it/wp-content/uploads/2017/10/Regolamento-Risiko.pdf)

   - The **defender** rolls **up to 2 dice**, regardless of how many armies are defending, unless the defender has decided to use a special rule (in some editions, 3 dice are allowed when defending a territory with ≥3 armies). [cdn.1j1ju](https://cdn.1j1ju.com/medias/ad/d9/a3-risk-rulebook.pdf)

3. **Compare the dice**

   - Sort attacker’s dice **descending**.  
   - Sort defender’s dice **descending**.  
   - Compare the **highest vs highest**, then **second highest vs second highest** (if both rolled at least 2).  

   - For each pair:
     - If **attacker’s die ≥ defender’s die**, the **defender loses 1 army**.  
     - If **attacker’s die < defender’s die**, the **attacker loses 1 army**. [cdn.1j1ju](https://cdn.1j1ju.com/medias/ad/d9/a3-risk-rulebook.pdf)

   Example (simplified):  
   - Attacker rolls: 6, 4, 2  
   - Defender rolls: 5, 3  
   - Compare: 6 vs 5 → defender loses 1; 4 vs 3 → defender loses 1 → total 2 armies lost by defender.  

4. **Repeat the battle**

   - After each roll, the attacker can:
     - Continue attacking from the **same territory** into the **same target** (if still at least 2 armies in the attacker’s territory and the defender still has armies), or  
     - Stop the attack and move to another battle. [media.wizards](https://media.wizards.com/2015/downloads/ah/Risk_rules.pdf)

5. **Capture a territory**

   - If the defender loses its **last army** in a territory, that territory is **captured**.  
   - The attacker must **move at least as many armies into the conquered territory as were used in the last attack**.  
     Example: You attacked with 3 armies and won; you must move **at least 3** from the attacking territory into the new one. [risiko](https://risiko.it/wp-content/uploads/2017/10/Regolamento-Risiko.pdf)

   - You can move **more** than that, as long as at least 1 army remains in the original territory.  

6. **End of attack**

   - You can **stop attacking at any time** during your attack phase. [media.wizards](https://media.wizards.com/2015/downloads/ah/Risk_rules.pdf)

## 8. Phase 3: Fortification (strategic move)

At the end of your turn, you may perform **one** fortification move. This is optional. [risiko](https://risiko.it/wp-content/uploads/2017/10/Regolamento-Risiko.pdf)

- You can **move armies** from **one of your territories** to **another of your territories**, **if they are connected by a continuous chain of your own territories**. [media.wizards](https://media.wizards.com/2015/downloads/ah/Risk_rules.pdf)

- You must **leave at least 1 army** on the territory you move **from**.  

- You can move **any number of armies** (as long as you leave at least 1) in this single move. [risiko](https://risiko.it/wp-content/uploads/2017/10/Regolamento-Risiko.pdf)

Example:  
- You have 8 armies in Europe and 3 in Asia, and both are connected through your own territories.  
- You can move 5 armies from Europe to Asia, leaving 3 in Europe.  

***

## 9. Risk cards and drawing

- After you **capture at least one territory** in your turn, you receive a **Risk card** from the deck. [media.wizards](https://media.wizards.com/2015/downloads/ah/Risk_rules.pdf)

- You may **never hold more than 5 Risk cards** at the end of your turn.  
  - If you already have 5 and you capture a territory, you **must** immediately trade in a set of 3 cards (and can then draw a new card after the trade). [risiko](https://risiko.it/wp-content/uploads/2017/10/Regolamento-Risiko.pdf)

- If the deck runs out, the **discard pile** is shuffled and becomes the new deck. [media.wizards](https://media.wizards.com/2015/downloads/ah/Risk_rules.pdf)

***

## 10. Winning conditions

There are two main ways to win, depending on the variant you choose before the game:

### 10.1. Classic “Eliminate All Opponents”

- Win by **eliminating all armies of one or more opponents** from the board. [wikihow](https://www.wikihow.com/Play-Risk)
- The last player with armies remaining wins. [wikihow](https://www.wikihow.com/Play-Risk)

### 10.2. “Secret Mission” Risk

Each player receives a **secret mission card** at the start (not revealed to others). [cdn.1j1ju](https://cdn.1j1ju.com/medias/ad/d9/a3-risk-rulebook.pdf)

Common missions include:
- Eliminate a specific color.  
- Control a certain number of territories (e.g., 18).  
- Control certain continents. [cdn.1j1ju](https://cdn.1j1ju.com/medias/ad/d9/a3-risk-rulebook.pdf)

- The **first player** who secretly completes their mission **and** meets the condition (e.g., controls the required territories) **wins immediately**, even in the middle of a turn. [cdn.1j1ju](https://cdn.1j1ju.com/medias/ad/d9/a3-risk-rulebook.pdf)

## 11. Basic additional rules and clarifications

- **Always leave at least 1 army**:  
  - No territory can be left empty.  
  - You may never attack from a territory that has only 1 army. [risiko](https://risiko.it/wp-content/uploads/2017/10/Regolamento-Risiko.pdf)

- **No attacks during fortification**:  
  - Once you decide to make a strategic move, your turn is over; you cannot attack in the same turn after fortifying. [media.wizards](https://media.wizards.com/2015/downloads/ah/Risk_rules.pdf)

- **Dice‑rolling order**:  
  - Generally, the attacker rolls first, then the defender, although the result is symmetric as long as both parties reroll when needed. [cdn.1j1ju](https://cdn.1j1ju.com/medias/ad/d9/a3-risk-rulebook.pdf)

- **Multi‑player attacks**:  
  - Only **two players** are involved in a single battle: one attacker, one defender.  
  - Other players do not participate in the dice‑rolling. [cdn.1j1ju](https://cdn.1j1ju.com/medias/ad/d9/a3-risk-rulebook.pdf)

***

## 12. Optional variants (short summary)

Some official rulebooks include these variants; you can agree on them before the game: [cdn.1j1ju](https://cdn.1j1ju.com/medias/ad/d9/a3-risk-rulebook.pdf)

- **Secret Mission Risk**: Each player has a hidden mission; first to complete wins.  
- **Capital Risk**: Each player selects a “capital” territory; if it is captured, that player is eliminated.  
- **2‑player rules**: Special setup, different starting armies, and sometimes special dice‑rolling rules. [cdn.1j1ju](https://cdn.1j1ju.com/medias/ad/d9/a3-risk-rulebook.pdf)
